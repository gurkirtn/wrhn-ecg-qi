# Product requirements

## Clinician

- Submit an anonymized ECG and clinician interpretation.
- Compare it with simulated AI output.
- Accept the AI result, retain the clinician result, or request expert review.
- View only their submitted cases and learning feedback.

## Expert reviewer

- See only cases sent for expert adjudication.
- Review highest-severity cases first.
- Record a final diagnosis, rationale, and learning takeaway.
- View aggregate quality-improvement analytics.

## Guardrails

- No patient-identifying information.
- AI is a second-reader aid, not autonomous clinical authority.
- All demo data and waveforms are synthetic.
